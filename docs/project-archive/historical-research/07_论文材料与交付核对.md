## 可用于 Results 的英文草稿

We evaluated fine-grained provenance detection under a source-group-disjoint HART protocol. Across three seeds, RoBERTa with cross-entropy and supervised contrastive learning achieved a mean Macro-F1 of 0.9115, compared with 0.9037 for RACE under the same loss combination. These results do not establish a general advantage of the discourse-graph architecture. Differences in pooling, parameterization, and access to full-document structure remain potential confounds.

In a post-hoc transfer extension, we froze a conservatively mapped subset of MixSet before model inference. After removing ambiguous source families and duplicate texts, the subset contained 359 texts from 91 source groups. A single RoBERTa cross-entropy checkpoint achieved a four-class Macro-F1 of 0.3901, versus 0.9075 on the grouped HART test set. The corresponding TF-IDF scores were 0.3163 and 0.7940. This subset is not equivalent to the original MixSet binary benchmark, and the transfer results should not be interpreted as a comparison with its published leaderboard.

We selected a threshold using only negative creator examples in the HART validation set, targeting an empirical false-positive rate of at most 1%. Applying the locked RoBERTa threshold to MixSet yielded an actual false-positive rate of 16.4% and a true-positive rate of 6.42%. On HC3, the same threshold yielded a false-positive rate of 0.6% and a true-positive rate of 5.8%. These observations indicate that a validation-selected operating point does not guarantee either low false alarms or useful recall under distribution shift.

数据证据：原始报告、`transfer_tfidf_v1/summary.json`、`transfer_roberta_v1/summary.json`；新结果单种子，不能与三种子均值混写。

## 可用于 Limitations 的英文草稿

The labels encode recorded dataset provenance rather than independently verified real-world authorship. In particular, the HART humanized class includes both tool-mediated and human editing. All 15,984 retained examples were consistent with the original metadata under our matching rules, but this does not recover unobserved editing histories. Our transfer subset uses an operational label mapping and has only 91 source groups; semantic near-duplicates and pretraining contamination have not been ruled out. Threshold calibration reused a validation partition previously used for checkpoint selection. Source-cluster bootstrap intervals condition on fixed checkpoints and thresholds and therefore exclude training-seed and calibration-selection uncertainty. Controlled matched-window graph ablations and held-out-domain retraining remain future experiments.
