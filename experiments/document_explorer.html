About this page
A local document-explanation workbench for the EE6405 text-provenance project. Everything runs on your machine.
What it does
Classifies an English document into four writing categories, then measures how each discourse unit affects one selected class score.
How to use it
Paste or import text, pick a model, then choose Analyze document. Switch to Influence, Coverage or Structure to inspect the same prediction.
Four models
TF-IDF, RoBERTa, RACE and RACE without contrastive loss. Each is an independent classifier; scores are never fused.
Reading the output
Whole-document scores on the right never change. Left-side colors show the effect of suppressing a unit on the selected class, not a passage label.
Privacy
Text goes only to the local service on this machine. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent to a third party.
Limits
Scores are learned patterns, not calibrated authorship probabilities or proof of who wrote the text.
